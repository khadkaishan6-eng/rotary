A fun filled Tihar cultural activities “Deusi Bhailo Program” was organized by Rotaract together with RC Kasthamandap on 23rd October 2022 at the residence of PP Rtn Prabhu Ram Bhandary at Koshi Compound Dillibazar, Kathmandu. The program included various activities like singing songs, deusi bhailo songs, single and group dances as well as magic by Rtr Pratham Oli. Magic show was extra ordinarily appreciated by all participants. President Yadab Pradhan and Rtr President Prabisha gave vote of thanks to PP Prabhu Ram Bhandary and Rtn Rita Bhandary for providing their nice garden for the venue. Presidents also thanked IPP Nepal Bhushan, PP Milan Bikram Shah, VP Dr Manohar, PP Gyanendra, Bhandary families as well as attendance of many Rotaract and their friends around 50. President Yadab appreciated RCK Rotaract for organizing such a wonderful event and said that such event helps in maintaining our cultural heritage and also encourages young people to join Rotaract and Rotary clubs. Program ended with handover of token of love. Cold drinks and snacks were also served. Rotaract also raised some funds for their social programs during the occasion.
