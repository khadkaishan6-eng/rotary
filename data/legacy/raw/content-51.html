RC Kasthamandap has been continuously supporting disabled children in Mulghat Dhankuta since last 16 years with the Rotary club Kasthamandap member’s contribution lead by PP Niranjan Bhattarai. This year also on 22nd December 2022 (7th Poush 2079) Past President Bhattarai visited Mulghat, Dhankuta and provided warm clothes, sports items, stationery as well as food items to 15 students including a helper. The distribution program was held at Sri Kausika Adhabhut School at ward# 9, Dha.Na.Pa. with the attendance of 150 people including students, teachers chaired by Subarna Karki from Dhankuta Nagarpalika and Special guest Govinda Tamang chairman of Teachers associations. The project cost was approximately Rs. 125,000.
