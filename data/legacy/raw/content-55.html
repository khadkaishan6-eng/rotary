RC Kasthamandap provided wheelchairs and warm cloths to disable people in Bhakhari and Jhorahat Morang District. The distribution program was held on 11th February 2023 at Jhorahat and on 12th February 2023 at Bhakhari by PP Niranjan Bhattarai in two different locations. There were about 30 local people participated in both places including ward Chairman of Bhakhari Mr. Hira Lal Kamat and ward Chairperson Mrs Durga Devi Sardar from Jhorahat Morang. Total four wheelchairs and warm cloths worth Rs. 20,000 were distributed which were supported by PP Niranjan, Rtn Lata Rajak and RCK club members.
