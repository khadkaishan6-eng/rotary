SERVICE ABOVE SELF – Exemplary Achievement of Rc Kasthamandap 


               
  
            Rtn. Yadab Pradhan 
Dear readers:  
The Rotary motto Service Above Self conveys the humanitarian  spirit and exemplary achievement 
has been set by the members of the Rotary club of Kasthamandap by being instrumental player in 
providing a very sophisticated cancer detecting Diagnostic Ultrasound Tomography Machine worth 
USD 400,000 to the Ibaraki Children Hospital, Ibaraki Japan for the children effected by the tsunami , 
earthquake and nuclear plant leakage  in March 2011 . The machine was purchased with  the 
contributions of the Rotary club of Kasthamandap, Rotary club of Shimodate in cooperation with 
Rotary International and Hitachi Medical Corporation.  This month four members from our club made 
a goodwill visit to the hospital in Japan to mark the occasion and to express the goodwill gesture as 
well as to cherish the  Nepal-Japan Friendship and International U nderstanding. Congratulations and 
many thanks to all those involved in this great work. 
 
I take this opportunity to wish you and your family a very healthy and prosperous life on the occasion 
of Dipawali festival.

Press Release in Japan and Nepal  
Tokyo, 5th October 2012  
 
 . 
 
 
 
 
 
 
                                                                                                                                                                
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Four member goodwill delegation from the 
Rotary c lub of Kasthamandap consisting 
Charter President Gajendra Bahadur 
Shrestha, Past President Umesh Shrestha, 
Secretary General Sajjan B.S. Thapa and 
Women Development Director Barsha 
Shrestha visited last year's 3/11 triple 
disaster tsunami, earthquake and n uclear 
plant leakage site in north eastern coast of 
Japan. Today, the delegates visited tsunami 
effected Ibaraki Children Hospital, where a 
very sophisticated cancer detecting 
Diagnostic Ultrasound Tomography 
Machine worth US$ 400,000, which was 
procured b y Rotary club of Kasthamandap 
and Rotary club of Shimodate in cooperation 
with Rotary International and Hitachi 
Medical Corporation. During a brief 
program, Charter President Gajendra 
Bahadur Shrestha said that this project is the 
reflection of deep love a nd respect of 
Nepalese people to the people of Japan and 
expressed that this goodwill gesture will be 
cherished forever as a symbol of Nepal -
Japan Friendship and International 
Understanding.  
Besides meeting with Mayor of Chikusei-
city Mr. Yoshizawa and Governor of Ibaraki 
Prefecture Mr. Hashimoto, the delegates 
accompanied by the Nepalese Ambassador 
in Japan H.E. Dr. Madan K. Bhattarai, 
former Japanese Ambassador to Nepal Mr. 
Tatsuo Mizuno and Shimodate Rotarians 
also paid a courtesy call to the former Prime 
Minister of Japan Hon'ble Taro Aso. During 
the meetings, the delegates highlighted about 
that the people to people relation could be a 
golden bridge, besides bilateral cooperation, 
in strengthening cordial ties between Nepal 
and Japan.
