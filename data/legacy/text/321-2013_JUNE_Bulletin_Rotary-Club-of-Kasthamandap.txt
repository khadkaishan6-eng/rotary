Rotary Information: 
 From Rotary.org 
  
Rotary is a volunteer organization with 33,000 
clubs in over 200 countries and geographical 
areas. It initiates humanitarian projects that 
address today’s challenges affecting the world 
today, such as hunger, poverty, and illiteracy. 
 
Rotary club members represent a cross-section of 
business and professional leaders around the 
world. These 1.2 million men and women donate 
their expertise, time, and funds to support local and 
international projects that help people in need and 
promote understanding among cultures. Rotary 
International is the worldwide association of Rotary 
clubs united under a motto of Service Above Self. 
 
Rotary’s flagship program is its effort to protect 
children against polio. It aims to eradicate the 
disease from the world.

Rotaract Kasthamandap News 
District Awards Pokhara, June 8 2013 
Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap participated in District Awards on June 8, 2013 in Pokhara. The event was 
hosted by Rotaract Club of Annapurna, Pokhara in Pokhara City hall. We bagged best award for Inter City Youth 
Exchange Program with Rotaract Club of Chi twan, our Twin club. Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap on the very 
occasion signed Twin club MOU with Rotaract Club of Pokhara Fishtail and also continued Twin relation with 
Rotaract Club of Chitwan. President 2013/14 Rtr. Amish Dhungel, Rtr Bikram Limbu, Pres ident 2012/13 Rtr 
Subigya S Shrestha, DIR 2013/14 Itr Rojina Duwal, Rtr Rajan Thapa, Rtr Anand Maharjan, Rtr Sakar Khatiwada 
and Rtr Binod Gautam were present on the occasion from Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap.

Orientation to BOD of Interact Club of Kasthamandap: Little Angels School 
Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap coordinated with the school administration of Little Angles’ school for the

Rotary Club of Kasthamandap Installation 
Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap participated in the Installation ceremony of the P arent club held on June 27, 2013

Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap Installation 
Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap held its 7th Installation in Little Angles School on June 29, 2013 with a participation 
of about 125 guests and audiences. DG Rtn Dilendra Raj Shrestha honored the program as chief guest. Similarly 
President 2012/13 Rtn Milan Bikram Shah, President 2013/14 Rtn Upendra  Poudyal and Past President Rtn P.B 
Limbu graced the program with their presence.  Rtn Milan congratulated the team for successful completion of the 
program and encouraged the new team and Rotaractors with moral wisdom. President Rtn Upendra committed to 
work as a team for the whole Rotary year 2013/14. President Rtr Amish in his acceptance speech said the Rotaract 
Kasthamandap will go on scaling new heights for the professional development of the members and for bring 
about a positive changes in the society. Annual theme “Conceive, Believe, Achieve” was released on the same day.
