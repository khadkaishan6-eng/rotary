Kasthamandap Service Project at Panchkhal School 
 
 

               
  
                      Rtn. Yadab Pradhan 
Dear readers:  
 
Rotary club of Kasthamandap members have been very active in planning and executing various project 
activities this month. A health camp at Shree Indrenee Vidya Mandir Panchkhal to the school students and 
villagers was conducted together with several other social service activities at the school such as water tank 
handover, book handover, cash handover to the toppers of each class. A re-cycle paper making project was 
also started this month with the initiative of club members that will benefit poor and unemployed women 
who are now learning to utilize the waste paper to make various useful products. A club can be vibrant only 
when the club can engage their members in social projects and these exemplary activities proves the 
pinnacle of the club and the reason for their members to join the club. Well done! And Congratulations! to 
each one of you. With the same vision three new members have joined the club this month and were 
inducted by PDG Rtn. Thirtha Man Shakya. Welcome! to the club.

In coming AG conducted induction for three new 
members. Rtn. Kamlesh introduced Rajkumar and 
Shiva and Rtn. Barsha introduced Rajju. After the oath 
new members were pinned by PDG Rtn. Tirtha man.

PDG Rtn. Tirthaman gave remarks as chief guest of 
the meeting, he said rotary clubs need to do more 
and working club is important against the number 
of rotary clubs. In his opinion it is high time the 
Rotary Clubs in Nepal merge among themselves to 
do meaningful service in the community rather than 
increasing club numbers. He said, among the 20 
percent of "fully functional" rotary clubs of Nepal 
RCK fall in that percentage.
