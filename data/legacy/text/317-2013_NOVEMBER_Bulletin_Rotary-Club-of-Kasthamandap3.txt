 From Rotary.org 
  ROTARY CLUBS TAKE ACTION FOR TYPHOON-RAVAGED PHILIPPINES 
Rotary clubs around the world are pledging emergency aid to communities in central Philippines after last week’s 
massive typhoon flattened entire coastal towns and villages, killed thousands of people, and displaced nearly 
600,000 more. 
The situation remains dire as widespread destruction has made food, water, and medicine scarce in remote areas 
affected by Typhoon Haiyan, the strongest storm to make landfall on record. 
Rotary President Ron Burton is urging our 34,000 clubs worldwide to continue to assist storm victims. 
"I know we all want to help. I am urging our clubs to take action to provide emergency aid now and begin planning 
for the future when we can help rebuild homes, schools, and businesses," says Burton. "We are in the business of 
helping people in need." 
Rotary partner ShelterBox has committed aid for 4,000 families in the form of emergen cy shelter and other relief 
assistance. 
Such disasters are “exactly why we entered into our partnership with ShelterBox,” says Burton. “It gives Rotary 
members worldwide the opportunity to respond immediately and in a very meaningful way to the life -threatening 
conditions faced by the people of the Philippines.” 
For nearly 100 years, Rotary clubs in the Philippines have been creating positive change in their communities. The 
first Philippine Rotary club was formed in Manila in 1919. Today, more than 800 Rotary clubs throughout the 
Philippines give members the chance to make a difference at home and around the globe.

# GM 551: 
 
 President Rtn. Poudyal welcomed the guest Hon’ble Mr. 
Gagan Thapa and thanked him for the time.  Rtn. Sajan 
introduced the guest.  Hon’ble Gagan Thapa thanked for 
giving him an opportunity to visit RCK.  Mr. Thapa talked 
about constitution drafting and the mistakes we all made 
from 2017 till date.  He said that there were big challenges 
ahead even within his party.  He was in favor of primaries 
like in the USA for electing leaders within his party.  He 
said that constitution is a process that changes with 
changes in society.  He said we should concentrate on 
present rather that for 100 years.  He talked about 
federalism and informed that NC was against ethnicity 
based state. He said that in order for our country to 
develop the following should be taken in account: 
 Rule of law 
 Good Governance 
 Gov and Private sector partnership 
 Make people important 
 Indian Nepal relation 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap Eventful Month: November 
 
 
 
 
  
  
 
 Deusi-Bhailo Program 
Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap organized Dheusi-Bhailo program 
this year also  on 4th November. Rotaractors play Dheusi -Bhailo in 
resident of Rotarians. Basically a fundraising event to support the 
Community Service Project of the club, Dheusi -Bhailo also provides a 
platform to share wishes, love and fellowship among Rotaractors, 
Rotarians, and Interactors making Partner In Service ever strong and lively. 
The event is organized every year to mark celebration of our 
traditional culture. It is also used as a platform to strengthen 
relationship between Rotary and Rotaract club members. 
Rotaractors visited residents of President Rtn. Upendra Poudyal, 
President Nominee Rtn. Prabhuram Bhandary and Rtn. Shiva 
Shrestha. It was a wonderful experience to exchange greetings with 
Rotarians in their residents.  
 
Joint Meeting with Interact club of Kasthamandap 
A joint meeting was organized with sister organization : Interact 
Club of Kasthamandap - Little Angles’ School. Members from both 
the club discussed about the joint projects that could be organized 
in days to come. Similarly President R tr. Amish took an update from 
President Itr. Xering about the Palpa Project th at the club is going to 
organize in Palpa. Rtr. Xering Tashi (PP of IC Kasthamandap)
