Rotary Information: 
 
 From Rotary.org 
  
Indian philanthropist and businesswoman Rajashree Birla has announced a new 
gift of US$1 million to Rotary to help eradicate polio. The gift brings her total 
contributions to the Rotary effort to more than $7.2 million. 
What’s more, this gift will be matched two -for-one by the Bill & Melinda Gates 
Foundation, resulting in $3 million in new funding for the  Global Polio 
Eradication Initiative (GPEI). Rotary is a leading partner in the GPEI, especially in 
advocating government and donor support, fundraising, and building public 
awareness. Through the  End Polio Now: Make History Today  fundraising 
campaign, the Gates Foundation is matching two -for-one every new dollar Rotary 
commits to polio eradication, up to $35 million per year, from 2013 through 2018. 
“Mrs. Birla’s generous gift is deeply appreciated and could not have come at a more opportune time in our 
fight to end polio once and for all,” says Ashok Mahajan, a former Rotary Foundation trustee and close 
friend of the Birla family. “Her generosity no doubt will inspire other private donors to step up and help 
Rotary take full advantage of our innovative partnership with the Gates Foundation.”

Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap Eventful Month: September 
 
 
 
 
  
 
 
Clean Up Bagmati:  RAC Kasthamandap 
together with Clean up Nepal organized 
Cleaning campaign in  Bagmati Banks . The 
project was the joint effort of Small Earth 
Australia and Clean up Nepal. Rotaract Nepal 
was a National partner while RAC 
Kasthamandap approached form Zonal level as a 
local partner. Along with President Rtr. Amish, 
DRR/PP Rtr Ashish, t he program observed the 
participations of 1 5 members form RAC 
Kasthamandap. Similarly  IPP of RC 
Kasthamandap Rtn Milan Bikram Shah was also 
present on the occasion  with his better half . The 
event also witnessed the participation of 
different social clubs a nd institutions like: St. 
Xavier school, Art of Living, Jagadamba Group 
etc. Altogether 33 Rotaract Clubs throughout the 
country had participated in the event in different 
locations. 
 
7th Installation of IC Kasthamandap:  
Little Angles’ School : Rotaract Kasthamandap 
helped in the installation ceremony of its sister 
organization. Inspite of the strike enforced by 
some parties, the installation ceremony was 
organized successfully. IPP Rtn. Milan Bikram 
blessed the occasion as a Chief Guest. He 
encouraged the Interactors to live a life of value 
and wisdom. He praised the TEAM of RY 
2012/13 for successful completion of the tenure 
and blessed the upcoming TEAM to come out  
with flying colors. Cultural Performances, 
singing, etc were the major attracti ons of the 
programs. 
 
Zonal Meeting Participation:   The club 
participated in 3 rd Zonal Meeting hosted by 
Rotaract Club of Bagmati in SMS Clinic in 
Kamalpokhari. Discussions about participation 
in PS Meet, District focus areas and further 
leadership of the Zone was made.  
 

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
End Polio Campaign : Rotaract Club of 
Kasthamandap continued the End Polio 
Campaign : World’s Largest Commercial. The 
faces of the month were : Rtn Milan Bikram 
Shah and Rtr Rajan Thapa. All together totals of 
46 photos have been uploaded in RI Website 
ranking RID 3292 in 33rd Position. 
 
26th Assembly of Partner Organisation: 
NAWB: During the program, Nepal Association 
for the Welfare of the Blind presented a token of 
appreciation to Rotaract Club of Kastham andap 
for its support for the welfare of the visually

Rotaract Club of Kasthamandap has helped in 
the successful implementation of the 
Association projects throughout the country by 
mobilizing the Rotaract club loc ated in the

PS Meet Bharatpur : Ro taract club of
