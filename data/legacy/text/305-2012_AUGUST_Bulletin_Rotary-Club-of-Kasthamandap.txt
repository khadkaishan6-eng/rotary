Dear readers:  
 
This month we have been blessed by the  visit of our DG Rtn Yogendra Man Pradhan and we are all inspired 
by his kind words which have energized us to get involved more actively in Rotary services. W e thank him 
and accompanying District Secretary Rtn. Mahendra Rajkarnikar for their precious time with us. 
 
This is the month of membership extension and we should all try our best to bring -in new member Rotarians 
to join our campaign to fulfill the mission of providing services to others, promote integrity and advance 
world understanding, goodwill and  peace. It is our duty to let our new members enjoy their partnering with 
us as well as encourage and help retain the existing members  through Rotary fellowship. It is also a time to 
rejoice for the award from Rotary International for the achievement being  the highest Membership Retention 
rate in District 3292during 2011-2012. 
  
Welcome! to all new members of our club.  
As a new member, you'll get a chance to become more involved in our  
community and to provide aid to other parts of the world through the 
opportunities that our Rotary club and Rotary International offer. 
Longtime Rotarians agree that involvement is  the key to getting the most out of 
membership. Volunteer to serve on a committee that meets your interests, to be 
a greeter for weekly club meetin gs, or to join a service project team. These 
activities will help you get to know your fellow club members and better 
understand the work of Rotary. Good luck and all the best to you all.

Mission 
The mission of Rotary 
International is to provide 
service to others, promote 
integrity, and advance 
world understanding, 
goodwill, and peace 
through its fellowship of 
business, professional, 
and community leaders.  
 
Diversity and 
Rotary 
Rotary International 
recognizes the value of 
diversity within individual 
clubs. Rotary encourages 
clubs to assess those in 
their communities who are 
eligible for membership, 
under existing membership 
guidelines, and to 
endeavor to include the 
appropriate range of 
individuals in their clubs. A 
club that reflects its 
community with regard to 
professional and business 
classification, gender, age, 
religion, and ethnicity is a 
club with the key to its 
future.

The names of members those who paid the annual fee were read out, the members those who have not paid yet were 
requested to pay as soon as possible. The names of terminated members of RCK were read out. The meeting details 
were also read out for 3 meeting held for July and average attendance was 52.66%. Members those who have provided 
or helped to get advertisement in Club Journal were requested to arrange for the payment.  
 
The meeting discussed on taking the project developed by Rtn. Prabha as Club project and means to finance the same. 
Rtn. Prabha also displayed the products sample created by the females under skill base d project to produce product 
from waste papers.  PP Rajan Raut informed that US $ 400 was received from Rose International Fund TRIFC which is 
equivalent to Rs. 33,000/- the money shall be utilized for providing Scholarships and Dictionary to the students.  
       
      
     
   
 
Further to the discussion of making RCK more visible, the meeting thought of displaying RCK banner at Kastamandap 
Mandir at Basantapur and help maintain the Kastamandap mandir from which our club has been named, Rtn. Ashok 
informed the meeting that he went to visit the Kastamandap Mandir in Basantapur and found that it has not been 
properly maintained as it should have under the heritage. The committee to manage the mandir was in existence but not 
to avail. He said he would be meeting committee chairman on Sunday 26 Aug. and shall report the club about the 
meeting.  
 
The meeting also informed the house that RI half yearly dues Rs. 1, 22, 086/ - District dues 65,000/- and Rotary News 
due Rs. 48,000/- has been paid.   
  
      
  
  
  
On Aug 31, 2012 DG was briefed by the following Club Committee Chairs on RCK Plan and Program for 2012-13 
 
Dr. Umed raised the issue of Nepal Ambulance Service (NAS) -RI District MOU to operate Ambulance service, which is 
category A type of Ambulance in  Nepal but the commitment of the RI district not being fulfilled. DG and DS in this 
particular issue replied that they are very well aware about the Ambulance Service and would like to see it going smothly, 
Rtn. Manohar Das Mool being busy with Directory publication may not have the time to look but they mentioned they will 
put this in priority and start in due time.  
 

Similary, PP Rtn. Rajan raised the status of Shelter Box Project and Spinal Injury Project which were ongoing RCK project, 
taken over by District 3292 from RCK. RCK was implementing both the project successfully but after District taking over, 
it has vanished. The D G and DS in this respect responded, they may have to put this issue in the meeting of Council of 
Governors to know what exactly happened and it present status. Further both DG and DS emphasized that projects are to 
be implemented by Clubs not the District and assured that both the project shall be returned to RCK.  
 
In order to mark RCK 500 th Grand meeting – RCK President Rtn. Milan shall sponsor 500 th RCK meeting at Dhulikhel on 
Saturday 22 nd Sept 2012 with lunch. The spouse of members is also invited in t his function. Our regular meeting of 21st 
Sept shall be postponed for one day for this reason. The members shall visit blind hostel at Sanjivin School and Dhulikhel 
hospital on this day. To mark this occasion the meeting decided to form committee under the  leadership of CP Rtn. 
Gajendra Bdr. Shrestha, with members Rtn. Barsha, Rtn. Bijaya, Rtn. Sushma and Rtn. Bhawana . IPP Rtn. Niranjan is given 
the responsibility of “Surprise Program”.  
 
Introduction of Chief Guest – DG Rtn. Yogendra Man Pradhan was given by CP Rtn. Gajendra. DG gave remarks in the 
meeting, he narrated he had never heard such heart touching & beautifying introduction of his own. He further added 
RCK is without doubt a very vibrant and good club with all potential leaders of future Rotary. H e said he has no clue why 
the District took over shelter box & spinal injury project, further NAS -Rotary MOU activity delay, he said he will look into 
the matter and put it on right track. He also requested RCK members to come forward and participate in NA S Project to 
start with as well.  
 
He informed from Nov. 18 -21 RI President Rtn. Sakuji Tanaka will visit Nepal and on that function RI District 3292 shall 
award Vocational Award in Education to one person. The nomination letter will be send to all clubs. Th e foundation 
dinner shall be hosted and if any rotary member pays for PHF, major donor, PHF level I, II, III or more on or before Sept. 
30, 2012, they shall be decorated on that occasion. Vibrant & elite club like RCK which is very good in all spheres shou ld 
strive for MPHF level 4 donor and also come forward to host various District Functions.  
   
Rtn. Prabhu provided vote of thanks to the DG and DS on behalf of RCK. He expressed gratitude and appreciation for all 
the wisdom, new connotations, lesson to RCK  and its members and challenged all to live up to the expectation. He 
anticipated more visits by DG to our club. He thanked DG and DS for today’s presence and kind words.
