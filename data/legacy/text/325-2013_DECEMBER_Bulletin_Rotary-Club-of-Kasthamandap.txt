Rotary Information: From Rotary.org  
ROTARY HELPS CLOSE OUT THE YEAR WITH GAINS AGAINST POLIO 
When Julia Yank and a team of Rotary members and health workers entered Kaduna, Nigeria, 
to immunize children against polio they expected to encounter some tough situations. They 
found one in a mother of three who stubbornly refused to have her children vaccinated. 
"She argued with us for over 15 minutes," says Yank, a member of the Rotary Club of St. Clair 
County Sunset in O'Fallon, Illinois, USA. After the team e xplained to her the importance of 
what they were doing, she finally agreed to allow her children to be immunized."We were told 
later that she only consented because of the presence of the Rotarians. That moment, I realized 
the impact we can make," says Yank. 
This type of persistence by Rotary and its partners in the  Global Polio Eradication 
Initiative (GPEI) has helped reduce the number of polio cases in Nigeria by half, as compared 
to this time last year. Nigeria, Pakistan, and Afghanistan are the only countries in the world 
where the transmission of polio has never been stopped. Overall, polio cases in the three 
countries have decreased by 35 percent -- thanks in part to Rotary's advocacy with government 
and business leaders, PolioPlus grants, and mobilizing support on the ground. 
From celebrating milestones and responding to outbreaks to signing commitments and 
honoring our supporters, learn how the Global Polio Eradication Initiative made a difference in 
2013. 
A YEAR WITHOUT TYPE 3 POLIO 
The last case of type 3 wild poliovirus (WPV3) occurred in Nigeria on 10 November 2012. 
Rotary and its GPEI partners have helped reduce transmission of WPV3 to its  lowest levels 
ever. "Although it is too soon to say that WPV3 has been eradicated . . . the world has a unique 
opportunity to get rid of the second strain of wild poliovirus" (after WPV2), reports the GPEI.  Rotary Information:

Second Club assembly of Rotaract Club of

Similarly the club also got two new enthusiastic

Hiking to Chisapani, Nuwakot 
Club members marked for a hiking to Chisapani on 
December 15, 2013. A team comprising of 9 Rotaractors 
from the club marked the journey from  Sundarijal at 
2:30 pm on Firday  towards Chisap ani of Nuwakot 
district. After 3.5 hours of hike, finally chisapani  
welcomed us with cold and rainy weather. 
 
A night stay with much of fun it was. Three new 
members of the club also got a chance to interact and 
built good relationship with the other club members. 
Next morning was welcomed by a beautiful sunrise with 
a spectacular mountain range. Left to Kathmandu at 
11:00 am we reached to Sundarijal at 4:00 pm.

NID first Phase: Dec 21, 2013  
National Immunization Day was organized by Rotary 
District 3292 in collaboration with Nepal Government, 
and WHO. For the execution of the same, Rotary and 
Rotaract Clubs were placed in various locations 
throughout the different part of the country. 
Rotaract and  Rotary club of Kasthamandap was 
assigned in the Narayan Gopal Chowk for the NID. 
Rotaractors from the club organized the promotion 
campaign on Dec 20 th  and 21 st in and around 
Maharajgunj chowk, Chunikhel, Kanti Child Hospital, 
Sankhapark, Bansbari etc.  Rotaractors equipped with 
hand-mike went to different locations requesting 
parents and guardians to immunize their children 
against crippling polio.  Rotary President Rtn. Upendra 
encouraged the Rotaractors with his presence in the 
program. 
Similarly to promote the public awareness against polio, 
club members also placed a flex in the Narayan Gopal 
chock to click for “World’s Largest Commercial” of 
Rotary International” This Close” initiative. 
Second phase of the program will be organized on 
January 25, 2014. Awareness program is scheduled for 
January 24 and 25 in and around the Narayan Gopal 
Chowk area.
