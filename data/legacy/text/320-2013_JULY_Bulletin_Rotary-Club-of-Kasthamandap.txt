Rotary leadership 
 
Governance 
All Rotary clubs are members of the global 
association Rotary International, which is 
overseen by an elected RI president and Board of 
Directors. The RI Board is responsible for 
establishing policy for Rotary International. 
The Rotary Foundation is a charitable organization supported solely by 
voluntary contributions. Managed by a Board of Trustees and trustee chair, 
the Foundation provides financial support to help Rotary clubs and districts 
work together on meaningful, sustainable service. 
Rotary's general secretary manages the 800-member Rotary staff who serve Rotarians from RI World Headquarters in Evanston, 
Illinois, USA, and several international offices. The general secretary is a member of both the RI Board of Directors and The 
Rotary Foundation Board of Trustees. 
Every Rotary club and every district (groups of clubs) selects its own officers and enjoys considerable autonomy within the 
framework of Rotary’s constitution and bylaws.

The DG congratulated all presenters on the wonderful job. He said that RCK is very effective and vibrant club in all 
respect and the activities proved that it has membership diversity, wonderful service projects and leadership who are 
capable of being leaders beyond the club.  However he said that there was still some room to improve.   Under 
Administration he suggested that one member visit different clubs every week and he emphasized on using the 
member access by going to www.rotary.org. For membership the DG suggested to have guest speakers who could 
be potential members.  DG suggested bringing people from Print media as members, that way the club’s service 
projects would always be highlighted.  DG congratulated on the wonderful service project that RCK was involved.  
He said that Nepal ambulance Service started by Service Project Chair Rtn. Om Rajbhandari was very special and 
said that he had kept that project un der District goal.  He also mentioned that Manipal Hospital of Pokhara was 
ready to run the Nepal Ambulance service.   He reminded that Rotary is about Members, Service Projects and 
Rotary foundation.  At the end he informed the assembly that the District Rotary Foundation (DRFC) had approved 
the $5,000 from District Designated fund (DDF), and thanked all the chairs and the members and reminded 
everyone to donate cash rather than gifts. With all the above comment, DG kindly announced the closer of Club 
Assembly and handed over the meeting to President Rtn. Upendra.
